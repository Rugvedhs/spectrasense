# APC waiver request — send BEFORE submitting

**Why before:** MDPI's Terms and Conditions state that once a manuscript is
accepted it may no longer be withdrawn by the author, and that persistent
non-payment can lead to future submissions being suspended. Acceptance is the
commitment point, so the funding question has to be settled first and the answer
has to be in writing.

**To:** polymers@mdpi.com
**Subject:** APC waiver enquiry before submission — unfunded student author

---

Dear Polymers Editorial Office,

I am writing before submitting a manuscript to ask whether a full Article
Processing Charge waiver would be available, as I have no funding of any kind.

I am a high school student and the work was carried out independently, without a
grant, an institutional affiliation that participates in your Institutional Open
Access Program, or any laboratory budget. I am not in a position to pay the
CHF 2,700 charge, and I would rather establish that now than submit and find the
question unresolved at acceptance.

The manuscript is a benchmarking and uncertainty-quantification study of
machine-learned glass transition temperatures across 7,174 homopolymer repeat
units, resolved by backbone family. It reports how prediction error and interval
reliability depend on which backbone chemistry is present in the training data,
compares a Van Krevelen group-contribution baseline against descriptor-based
ensembles, and releases all code and derived data openly. I believe it fits the
journal's scope in polymer physics and computational polymer science.

I would be grateful if you could tell me:

1. whether a full waiver can be granted for an unfunded independent author;
2. if not, whether any partial reduction applies; and
3. whether such a decision can be confirmed in writing before I submit.

If no waiver is available I will submit elsewhere, with no hard feelings — I
simply want to avoid reaching acceptance with the charge unpayable.

Thank you for your time.

Yours sincerely,
[AUTHOR NAME]
[EMAIL]
[SCHOOL / CITY, COUNTRY]

---

## Notes before you send

- Send from the address you will use as corresponding author.
- Do not submit the manuscript until you have a written answer.
- If the reply is encouraging but vague ("waivers are considered case by case"),
  that is **not** a waiver. Ask again for a yes or no.
- Keep the reply. If a waiver is granted, quote it in your submission cover
  letter and in the comments-to-editor field.
- If the answer is no, JCIM (ACS) charges no APC at all and is the fallback.
